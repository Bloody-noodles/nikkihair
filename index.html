Meet your stylist

Where Skill Meets Passion

Innocent Nkeiruka Justina — professionally known as Nikihairstyles — is a professional hair stylist, wig expert and hair educator based in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State. She's built a name for ultra-long installs, melted glueless lace applications and transformations that make clients feel like the best version of themselves.

From 40″ buss downs to fresh layered bobs revived from old units, every style is finished with detail: clean parts, laid edges, and hair that moves naturally. She also teaches — sharing tutorials and behind-the-scenes of her journey as a beauty content creator.
